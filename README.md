# AD Prep

Offline study planner for Bangladesh Grade 9 Assistant Director exams.

**Open it:** https://mostofa625.github.io/ad-prep/. On your phone, use "Add to Home screen" so it works offline like an app.

- **Today** – the day's sessions as a checklist, this week's topics, mock score, cards due, exam countdown
- **Plan** – the 12-week plan (15 Oct 2026 – 6 Jan 2027), weekly topics, mock score history
- **Mistakes** – log wrong answers as flashcards; spaced review brings them back at the right time
- **Essays** – argument essay template, 12 practice prompts, 30-minute timer, saved essays
- **More** – exam dates (the plan switches to mocks 14 days before), viva practice notes, links, backup

Your data stays on your device. Use **More → Export backup** now and then.
